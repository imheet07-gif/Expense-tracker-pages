An expense tracker for your iPhone. See where your money goes, and log a spend in two seconds with a double tap on the back of your phone.

  • A dashboard with charts, a monthly budget and your spending by category
  • Split bills, send WhatsApp reminders for money owed, track loan EMIs and insurance
  • Import bank statements, and optionally log automatically from bank alert emails
What you need
  • A free Notion account. Your expenses are stored there, in your own tables.
  • The free Scriptable app from the App Store.
  • An iPhone and about 10 minutes. Nothing costs money.
Start setup Step by step, on your iPhone. Your data stays in your own Notion; nothing is stored on anyone else's server.
Open setup No Notion? Private edition Keeps everything in a file on your phone. No Notion, no Google. No automatic card alerts. Already set up? The Copy page has the latest code and what's new.
Questions

Does it cost anything? No. Notion's free plan, Scriptable and the dashboard are free.

Do I need Google? Only if you want automatic logging from bank alert emails. The core setup needs no Google account.

Who sees my data? Only you. It lives in your Notion, and the dashboard runs on your phone.
