Older updates

Old steps below. Many of these entries say "paste the new code". That is no longer needed: update the dashboard from Settings › Updates, and the Google scripts update themselves. This page is a record of what changed and when.

Version 7.14: new look, a bar you can swipe and smaller text (14 Oct)

  • The bottom bar slides sideways. Every tab is on it (Overview, Trends, Expenses, Events, People, and Loans when you have loans or card EMIs), so there is no More tab any more. Up to five tabs fill the width; with six, swipe the bar and the next tab peeks out at the edge. The tab you open is kept in view.
  • Text is one notch smaller across the dashboard (body text, big numbers and titles), so more fits on the screen.
  • Both only apply when the New look is switched on in Settings › Title.

Version 7.13: new look, the title is no longer cut off (14 Oct)

  • New look (beta): on some phones the title ended in dots ("Sam's ledg...") because it was fitted before its typeface had finished loading. It is now fitted again as soon as the typeface arrives, and a little after the screen first draws, so it shrinks to fit beside the time and the buttons. Nothing changes unless the New look is switched on in Settings › Title.

Version 7.12: new look, everything on one line (14 Oct)

  • New look (beta): your title, the "updated 12:01 PM" status, refresh and the gear now share a single line at the top, so the top of every screen is as compact as the old look again. A long title shrinks to fit, and a very long one is cut off with dots rather than running into the buttons. Nothing changes unless the New look is switched on in Settings › Title.

Version 7.11: new look, less empty space on top (14 Oct)

  • New look (beta): the big title now shares its line with the refresh button and the gear, and the "updated 12:01 PM" text moved under the title. That gives you about 60 px back at the top of every screen. Nothing changes unless the New look is switched on in Settings › Title.

Version 7.10: a new iPhone look (beta), off until you turn it on (14 Oct)

Nothing changes unless you switch it on: Settings › Title › New look (beta). Switch it off again any time to go back.

  • Bottom tab bar like other iPhone apps: Overview, Trends, Expenses, People and More (Events and Loans & insurance are inside More). Settings stays a gear at the top right of every screen.
  • Large titles: on Overview your own title (for example "Sam's ledger", in the same typeface as before) is the big title; on the other screens the screen's name is big with your title small above it. When you scroll, a small title appears at the top.
  • iPhone colours, font and shapes: true black in dark mode, soft grey in light mode, the iPhone system font, no outlines on cards, lighter buttons and chips. Colours were checked for readability in light and dark.
  • Phones only: iPad and computers keep their own layout. More of the new look (swipe actions, pull-down sheets) is coming.

Version 7.9: fixes "Alerts is not a property that exists" (13 Oct)

  • Review > Add on a payment not logged: if your Expenses table had no Alerts column (a table set up another way), adding a payment from an alert stopped with "Alerts is not a property that exists". The app now makes that column itself (or renames the link that is already there) and adds the expense. If it can't be made, the expense is still saved, just without the link. Your Google script does the same when it links an alert to an expense you logged.

Version 7.8: new banks are recognised more reliably (13 Oct)

  • New bank accounts from SMS: a bank that happens to end in the same digits as another of your cards or accounts is no longer mistaken for it, so it is added to Settings › Cards & bank accounts. An account you removed is remembered by its bank name, not just its digits.
  • Banks found by your Google script too: when a bank SMS or email reaches the CC Transactions table from a bank you haven't added, that bank's account is now added to Settings › Cards & bank accounts by the Google script as well (before, only the iPhone shortcut did it). An account you remove in the app is remembered in a "Removed accounts" line on the Card Bills description, next to "Removed cards".
  • The shortcut tells you what it did: after each bank SMS the reply says whether the account was added, was already listed, was one you removed, or could not be added and why. The same line is written to the log file.

Version 7.7: a full check-up, with the fixes (13 Oct)

Every part of the app was read through by independent reviewers who tried to break it. These are the real problems they found, now fixed, each with a test so it can't come back.

  • Statements: two identical purchases on the same day (two coffees) are both imported; a card refund written as -1,299.00 or (2,000.00) is a credit, not spending; a row marked Dr is spending whatever its words; UPI AutoPay subscriptions (Netflix, Spotify) are spending, not "credit card bill"; a "0.00" in the other column no longer loses the row; "balance forward" lines are not spending; statements listed newest first read correctly; impossible dates (31 Feb) are ignored; and a long PDF says if it had to stop reading.
  • Loans and insurance: "Cr" after an amount (a credit mark) is no longer read as crore; an insurance receipt now gives the next due date, not the one just paid; a loan table listed newest first anchors on the latest balance.
  • Trends and Overview: a category you added in Notion no longer breaks the charts; the 3-month average uses only the months you have; a budget in rupees is judged in rupees when you view another currency; a page left open overnight moves to the new day; the title buttons work on every tab; drafts of "Who owes me" and "Multiple categories" come back if a save fails; the People pay ledger counts from the day you start tracking and always reaches this month.
  • Log Expense shortcut: typed amounts like "Rs.450" or "450 for 2 people" are read correctly (they were logged as ₹0.45 and ₹4502); dates like "12/10/2026" or "Oct 12, 2026" work; categories match ignoring case.
  • Safer data: in the private edition, moving your data between iCloud and the phone, and restoring a backup, can no longer lose it (the old copy is kept until the new one is checked); a bank SMS whose save failed is retried instead of being forgotten; changing your Notion key also resets the table checks.
  • Small things: a bank SMS from a bank the app has never seen, charging a fee ("SMS charges", "GST", "Chrgs"), is now logged as that bank's charges under Bills & Utilities, with the bank and account digits in the note, instead of "Unknown" under Other; a bank the app has not seen before is also added by itself to Settings › Cards & bank accounts (an account you remove stays removed); there is a new payment method, Bank transfer (NEFT, IMPS, RTGS, net banking and "TRF" bank SMS, and bank fees), with its own filter chip, colour and place in the edit lists, so these are no longer filed as Debit Card or UPI; Add under Settings › Cards & bank accounts now says what is missing (the bank's name, or the last digits) instead of doing nothing, and if Notion refuses the change you are told why in a message in front of the Settings panel (it used to show only behind it); the bank list in that form is much longer (Bandhan, Standard Chartered, Citi, IDBI and more), on iPhone the form now updates when you choose from its lists (choosing Other shows the Bank name box; a bank account no longer shows a Card name box), and what you type there is no longer lost if the page refreshes while you tap Add; Esc no longer closes Settings while you are typing in a box; the Events tab fits the narrowest phones.

Version 7.6: made for iPad and computers (12 Oct)

Version 7.5: a quicker, clearer home-screen widget (12 Oct)

  • The widget does less work each time (this month and last month's expenses and your bank alerts, nothing else), so it finishes sooner and iOS can refresh it more often. If Notion or the alert check is slow it shows the last saved figures instead of waiting.
  • It now asks iOS to refresh every 10 minutes instead of 30. iOS still decides the real timing, and the widget now shows Updated 3:42 pm (or Saved with the time of the saved copy), so you always know how fresh it is.
  • Fixed: the large widget's "Recently logged" list stopped the widget from drawing at all. It works again.

Version 7.4: Card EMIs on the Loans tab (12 Oct)

  • Card EMIs: when you import a card statement that has an EMI (a purchase converted to EMI), the Loans tab now shows it: the item and card, how many instalments are paid, about how much you pay a month, how much is left, the month it ends, and the interest so far. It is read from the statements, nothing to type. If an instalment's statement hasn't been imported it says so, and finished EMIs fold away under "Finished". It changes none of your totals; the purchase still counts when you made it.

Version 7.3: PDF statements read properly (11 Oct)

  • Loan statements are read row by row, so the EMIs you really paid are found even when they changed: step-up and step-down plans, interest-only periods, EMI holidays, rate changes, part payments and a final balloon payment. A statement whose header says "Sanctioned" is no longer mistaken for a sanction letter. Bounced EMIs, late fees and interest that is only accruing are not counted as payments, and the lender's own balance on the last paid row is used.
  • Bank statements: loan EMI debits (NACH, ECS, EMI, loan account numbers) are filed under Bills & Utilities and credit card bill payments under Credit Card Dues, recognised by their wording. Look-alike names such as a cafe with "EMI" in it are left alone. (Linking them to your own loans comes in a later version.)
  • Credit card EMI conversions: a purchase you converted to EMI counts once, when you made it. The monthly instalments' principal is not counted again; the interest and fees still are. If the original purchase happened before you started importing statements, those instalments are not counted anywhere.
  • A repayment schedule (a plan from EMI 1 with no paid marks) is no longer treated as payments already made.

New: the Google scripts now update themselves (10 Oct)

  • Paste once, never again. The Card alerts sync and the Setup link code below is now a small loader. Better at reading loan and policy PDFs (7 Oct)

The PDF reader used to look only for "Label: value" on one line, so it missed figures laid out in tables. It now also reads:

  • Tables: a row of headings with the values on the row below (Policy No. · Start date · End date · Sum insured), and a label with its value on the next line.
  • Google's reader output: a block of labels followed by a block of values, and statement rows where the date isn't first. A loan statement now gives all of its EMIs instead of just 2.
  • Small print: footnote marks ("Interest Rate¹", "Regular EMI Amount⁴"), "Rs. 18,456/-", "₹1 Crore", "5 Lakh", dates like 12-Nov-26, LIC modes (YLY, HLY), receipt totals and payment dates.
  • It picks the regular EMI over the first one, and ignores phone and account numbers.
  • When nothing can be read on the iPhone, it offers to send the PDF to your Google script (which reads it a different way) instead of stopping at "nothing found".

Also fixes "daysInMonth is not defined", which stopped the insurance email step. Paste the new Expenses Dashboard (Settings should show version 6.1) and the new Card Alerts Sync (or shared-sheet edition), then run install once.

Open a policy and tap Add a document, or From PDF at the top of Insurance, then pick the policy schedule, a renewal notice or a receipt from Files.
