1 · Install the dashboard

Start here

This is the screen you open on your iPhone. New here? Follow the Setup guide first; it sets up your Notion and then sends you back to this button. Once it's installed, you never copy it again: update it from Settings › Updates inside the dashboard.

Steps and code preview

  2. In Scriptable, tap +, rename the script Expenses Dashboard, paste what you copied, and tap ▶.
  3. Already installed it? Don't paste again. Open Settings › Updates in the dashboard and tap Check for updates.

2 · Turn on card alerts

Optional

Every 10 minutes, new bank and card alerts from your Gmail are added to your Notion, matched to what you logged, and tagged with the right card. The dashboard shows any payment you haven't logged. Pick whichever way below suits you; the first is the easiest.

The easy way: your own copy of a Google Sheet

Make your own copy of the sheet
  1. Tap the button, sign in to your Google account, and tap Make a copy.
  2. In your copy, wait a few seconds for the Expense Tracker menu to appear next to Help.
  3. Tap Expense Tracker › Start, allow access when Google asks (tap Advanced › Go to… if it warns the app isn't verified), and paste your Notion key when it asks.
  4. That's it. Your cards are found automatically and the sheet shows what it found.

Use a browser, ideally on a computer: the Google Sheets phone app doesn't show the menu. On a phone, open the sheet link in Safari and choose "Request Desktop Website".

Were you sent a setup link?

If the person who shared हिशोब with you sent a setup link, open it on your phone. It does all of this for you and walks you through the shortcut and widget too. Use that one or the sheet above, not both.

Advanced: paste the code yourself

If you prefer your own Google Apps Script project, paste this small loader once. It downloads the real code, checks it, and keeps itself up to date.

  1. In Notion, open CC Transactions (Beta) › ••• › Connections and add the same integration your Expenses table uses.
  2. Open script.google.com (easiest on a computer; on a phone use Safari's "Request Desktop Website"). Tap New project, delete what's there, and paste the loader.
  3. Paste your Notion key (starts with ntn_) between the quotes on the NOTION_TOKEN line near the top. Save.
  4. Choose the function install in the toolbar and tap Run. Allow Gmail access when Google asks. It then runs every 10 minutes by itself.
  5. Clear the key from the NOTION_TOKEN line (leave the quotes) and Save. It's already stored privately.

Two switches at the top of the loader: AUTO_UPDATE (false stops all update checks) and COUNT_ME (false stops the anonymous once-a-day visit count). Your SMS-to-email automation stays as it is.

Private edition

No Notion, no Google

A separate copy of the dashboard if you want your data on your phone only. It reads credit card and bank statements (PDFs from net banking) and anything you log by hand. Update it from Settings › Updates, same as the main dashboard.

Steps and code preview
  1. Install Scriptable from the App Store.
  2. In Scriptable, tap +, rename the script Expenses Dashboard, paste what you copied, and tap ▶.
  3. To log by hand: use your Log Expense shortcut with Back Tap, or the dashboard's + Add expense.
  4. Each month: download your card and bank statements, then tap Import a statement and pick the PDFs. If a statement is password protected, it asks for the password. Spends you already logged by hand are matched, not added twice.
  5. Settings › Your data: choose This iPhone only or iCloud Drive, and Export a backup now and then. If data is kept only on the phone, deleting Scriptable deletes it.

Card payments, refunds and incoming money aren't counted as spending. ATM withdrawals are left out (log cash spends by hand). Scanned (image) statements can't be read. Card alerts and Gmail features aren't in this edition.

What's new

New: the app is called हिशोब (10 Oct)

  • हिशोब means "the accounts" in Marathi. The dashboard header now shows it in a bold Marathi display font (Rozha One) that is built into the app, so nothing is loaded from the internet. This works the same in the Private edition.
  • The Copy, Setup and front pages carry the same name and lettering.
  • Update from Settings › Updates, as before.

New: card alerts without any code (10 Oct)

  • Make your own copy of a Google Sheet, tap Expense Tracker › Start in its menu, paste your Notion key, and allow access. Your cards are found automatically and alerts start flowing. There is no code to paste.
  • It keeps itself up to date, so fixes reach you without any work from you.
  • You'll find the button under 2 · Turn on card alerts above. On a phone, open the link in Safari and choose "Request Desktop Website", because the Google Sheets app doesn't show the menu.

New: a clearer top bar, month and year pickers, and custom splits (10 Oct)

  • The top bar now says "Money" and holds only the tabs. The month no longer sits above tabs that don't use it.
  • Overview: the month is just under the tabs. Tap the month name to jump to any month: pick a year, then a month. On a past month a This month button takes you straight back.
  • Trends: one year choice at the top (Calendar or FY, with arrows). Tap the year to pick any year from a list. The year summary, both charts and the average card all follow it, so going back a year is one tap.
  • Expenses: tap the selected month chip to open the same month picker.
  • Splitting an expense: Edit › Split / owed now has Full, 90%, 75%, 50%, 25% and an Other % box for each person, a plain amount box for any custom figure, and Split the other ₹X equally for whatever is left. Your share always shows as what remains.
  • Update from Settings › Updates, as before.
