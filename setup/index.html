For iPhone

हिशोब Setup

Log a spend in two seconds with a double tap on the back of your phone, and see where your money goes on a home-screen dashboard. It also splits bills with the people you share them with, sends WhatsApp reminders for money owed, tracks loan EMIs (it finds your loans from your bank and lender emails), keeps a money diary with family and domestic help (pay, advances and days off), and keeps one-off trips out of your monthly numbers. Your data stays in your own Notion.

What you need

  • A free Notion account
  • The free Scriptable app from the App Store
  • About 10 minutes

Were you sent a setup link? Open it on your phone and sign in with Google instead. It walks you through everything below, one step at a time, and makes your Notion tables for you. You don't need this page.

1 · Set up Notion

About 3 minutes
  1. Open the Expense Tracker template and tap Duplicate.
  2. Go to notion.so/profile/integrations › New integration, name it Expense Tracker and save. Copy the Internal Integration Secret (it starts with ntn_). This is your Notion key.
  3. Open your new Expense Tracker page › ••• › Connections › add Expense Tracker.

2 · Install the dashboard

  1. Install Scriptable from the App Store and open it once.
  2. In Scriptable, tap +, name the script Expenses Dashboard, paste what you copied, and tap ▶.
  3. Paste your Notion key when asked. It finds your tables by itself. (Pasted the wrong one? Open Settings › Notion connection in the dashboard and paste a new key.)
  4. For the home-screen widget: long-press the home screen › + › Scriptable › add it, then tap the widget and choose Expenses Dashboard.

Later, update the dashboard from Settings › Updates inside it. You never need to copy it again.

3 · Add the double-tap shortcut

Add the Log Expense shortcut
  1. Tap the button. It asks for your Notion key once: paste the same key (it starts with ntn_) you used in step 2, then tap Add Shortcut. It finds your Expenses table by itself.
  2. Go to Settings › Accessibility › Touch › Back Tap › Double Tap and choose Log Expense.
  3. Double-tap the back of your phone, answer the few questions, and the spend is saved to your Notion.

The key stays inside the shortcut on your own phone. If you ever change your Notion key in the dashboard, change it in the shortcut too (open the shortcut, tap the first text box, paste the new key).

4 · Card alerts and bill checks

Optional

Reads your bank alert emails in Gmail. It works out your cards and accounts by itself, tags each expense with the card you used, tells you which payments you forgot to log, and checks every card bill for hidden fees.

Make your own copy of the sheet
  1. Tap the button, sign in with the Google account that gets your bank alert emails, and tap Make a copy.
  2. In your copy, wait a few seconds for the Expense Tracker menu to appear next to Help, then tap Expense Tracker › Start.
  3. Google warns that the app isn't verified, because it's a private script rather than a published app. Tap Advanced › Go to… and allow access.
  4. Paste your Notion key (the same ntn_ key as above) when it asks.

Use a browser, ideally on a computer: the Google Sheets phone app doesn't show the menu. On a phone, open the link in Safari and choose "Request Desktop Website". It then shows the cards it found and syncs every 10 minutes. Alerts from HDFC, Axis, ICICI, SBI, Kotak, Amex and Scapia are recognised. If your bank only sends alerts by SMS, you can forward them to your Gmail with an iPhone automation that sends an email titled [CC-SMS].

5 · Let it log for you

Optional

In the dashboard, tap the gear › Logging. Choose Fill in what I miss or Fully automatic, and expenses are added from your bank's alerts, with categories learned from what you pick. It works with the card alerts sheet above, or with just your bank SMS on the iPhone: the Logging section shows the 2-minute Shortcuts setup. It starts in preview, so you can see what it would add before switching it on.
