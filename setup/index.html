Expense Tracker Setup

Log a spend in two seconds with a double tap on the back of your phone, and see where your money goes on a home-screen dashboard. It also splits bills with friends, sends WhatsApp reminders for money owed, tracks loan EMIs (it finds your loans from your bank and lender emails), keeps a money diary with friends, family and domestic help (pay, advances and days off), and keeps one-off trips out of your monthly numbers. Your data stays in your own Notion.

Got a setup link from Heet? Use that instead

Open it on your phone and sign in with Google. It walks you through everything below, one step at a time, and makes your Notion tables for you. You don't need this page.

  1. Set up Notion

    Open the Expense Tracker template and tap Duplicate.

    Then go to notion.so/profile/integrations › New integration, name it Expense Tracker and save. Copy the Internal Integration Secret (it starts with ntn_). This is your Notion key.

    Last, open your new Expense Tracker page › ••• › Connections › add Expense Tracker.

  2. Install the dashboard

    Install Scriptable from the App Store and open it once. Then open the Expenses Dashboard file Heet sent you › Share › Save to Files › iCloud Drive › Scriptable.

    In Scriptable, tap Expenses Dashboard and paste your Notion key when asked. It finds your tables by itself.

    For the home-screen widget: long-press the home screen › + › Scriptable › add it, then tap the widget and choose Expenses Dashboard.

  3. Add the double-tap shortcut

    Open the shortcut link from Heet › Add Shortcut. There's nothing to fill in.

    Then Settings › Accessibility › Touch › Back Tap › Double Tap › choose Log Expense. Double-tap the back of your phone to log a spend.

Optional: card alerts and bill checks

Reads your bank alert emails in Gmail. It works out your cards and accounts by itself, tags each expense with the card you used, tells you which payments you forgot to log, and checks every card bill for hidden fees.

  1. Open the card alerts setup link Heet sent you. It works on your phone.
  2. Sign in with the Google account that gets your bank alert emails. Google warns that the app isn't verified, because it's a private script rather than a published app. Tap Advanced › Go to Expense Tracker and allow access.
  3. Paste your Notion key (the same ntn_ key as above) and tap Start.

On a computer you can instead copy the Card Alerts sheet and tap Expense Tracker › Start in its menu. Use one or the other, not both.

The page then shows the cards it found and syncs every 10 minutes. Alerts from HDFC, Axis, ICICI, SBI, Kotak, Amex and Scapia are recognised. If your bank only sends alerts by SMS, you can forward them to your Gmail with an iPhone automation that sends an email titled [CC-SMS].

Optional: let it log for you

In the dashboard, tap the gear › Logging. Choose Fill in what I miss or Fully automatic, and expenses are added from your bank's alerts with categories learned from what you pick. It works with the card alerts sheet above, or with just your bank SMS on the iPhone: the Logging section shows the 2-minute Shortcuts setup. It starts in preview, so you can see what it would add before switching it on.
