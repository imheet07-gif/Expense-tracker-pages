Ledger privacy policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Your money details stay on your phone. Ledger has no accounts, no servers of its own and no tracking.

What Ledger stores

Your expenses, the cards and bank accounts it learns about, your budget and your settings. All of it is kept on your iPhone, in the app's own storage. The developer cannot see it and does not receive it.

Bank messages

If you set up the Shortcuts automation, your iPhone hands the text of bank messages to Ledger. Ledger reads them on your phone to find the amount and the shop. The text is not sent anywhere. Messages that Ledger cannot understand are kept in a list on your phone. They leave your phone only if you open one and choose Share, after Ledger has hidden every digit and you have had the chance to edit it.

What we collect

Nothing. Ledger has no analytics, no advertising, no sign-in and no tracking.

Apple

If you installed Ledger through TestFlight, Apple may collect crash reports and any feedback you send through TestFlight, under Apple's own terms. If you use iCloud device backups, Apple may back up the app's data with the rest of your phone.

Importing from Notion (optional)

If you choose "Bring my data from Notion", you give Ledger your own Notion access key. Ledger uses it only to read your own tables, on your phone. It never writes to Notion.

Backups and exports

When you make a backup file or a CSV, it goes where you choose (Files, AirDrop, email). That is in your hands.

Deleting your data

Settings has a button that deletes everything Ledger holds. Deleting the app also removes its data from the phone.

Children

Ledger is not aimed at children.
